Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HumanMaxxed handles information on this public website and in connection with a future TikTok integration.

Effective September 10, 2026

On this page

Scope Information How we use it Sharing Retention Your choices Contact

1. Scope and current status

This Privacy Policy applies to the HumanMaxxed public website and to HumanMaxxed features that may connect to TikTok in the future. This website is a static informational site. It does not include analytics, advertising trackers, account registration, forms, cookies set by HumanMaxxed, or an active TikTok API connection.

The TikTok Developer integration is not configured yet. If it is activated, HumanMaxxed will request access only through TikTok’s authorization process and only after a user chooses to connect an account.

2. Information we may process

The public website does not directly collect personal information. Like other sites hosted through GitHub Pages, technical request data may be processed by the hosting provider under its own policies.

If a TikTok connection is introduced, the information available to HumanMaxxed will depend on the permissions a user approves. It may include:

  • TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information;
  • information about media a user selects or creates for an authorized publishing workflow;
  • publishing status and related media metadata; and
  • authorization tokens required to maintain the connection.

HumanMaxxed will not ask users to provide their TikTok password.

3. How information may be used

Information obtained through an authorized integration may be used to provide the feature the user requests, maintain and secure the connection, diagnose technical problems, prevent misuse, comply with applicable obligations, and communicate important service notices.

HumanMaxxed does not sell personal information and does not use TikTok user data for unrelated advertising.

4. When information may be shared

Information may be shared with TikTok when a user directs HumanMaxxed to perform an authorized action. It may also be processed by service providers that support hosting, security, or operation of the requested feature, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection requirements.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or support a business transfer. HumanMaxxed does not permit service providers to use personal information for their own independent marketing.

5. Retention and security

If the integration is activated, HumanMaxxed will retain personal information only for as long as needed to provide the authorized feature, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the service. Authorization tokens will be stored in protected server-side systems, never in this public website.

HumanMaxxed uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and requests

Users can decline a future TikTok connection or revoke it through TikTok’s settings. Where applicable, users may also ask HumanMaxxed to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of their personal information.

Revoking access stops new authorized access, but it may not automatically remove information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons. Requests will be evaluated under applicable law.

7. Children’s privacy

HumanMaxxed services are not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in a user’s jurisdiction. HumanMaxxed does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this public website.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as HumanMaxxed features develop or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate service notice when required.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official contact method shown in the HumanMaxxed application or its TikTok application listing. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials in a support request.
